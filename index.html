Welcome to Supremacy College Zambia

We aim to empower learners with relevant knowledge, professional values and practical abilities that support employment, self-reliance and lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in Zambia, recognised for quality education, practical skills and academic excellence.

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable education and practical training that empower learners for employment, entrepreneurship and national development.

Our Objective

To equip learners with relevant knowledge, practical skills and professional values that promote employability, self-reliance and lifelong learning.

Admissions

Interested in studying with us? Contact the college to confirm the programme, current intake dates, fees and entry requirements, then request an application form.

Programme Durations

  • Most diploma programmes: 2 years, as listed.
  • Health Sciences diploma programmes: 3 years, as listed.
  • Certificate programmes: 1 year, as listed.

Before You Apply

  • Confirm that your chosen programme is currently offered.
  • Ask about entry requirements and required documents.
  • Confirm tuition fees and payment arrangements.
  • Ask about current intake and application deadlines.
